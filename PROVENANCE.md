# Provenance

The initial Sangrep Workbench guides were authored for Sangrep and adapted into this repository by
the copyright holder. They describe only the public-safe projection recorded in their page metadata.
No customer material, private document, provider payload, third-party user guide, or product
screenshot is included.

Renderer, policy, test, and verification files are repository-owned work contributed under the terms
in LICENSE and LICENSE_MAP.json. Dependencies are installed from the exact npm lockfile and retain
their upstream licenses; no dependency source is copied into this repository.

`public/media-manifest.json` is the authority for screenshot and recording provenance. Its current
empty inventory is intentional because no reviewed product media is eligible for public history.
Generated static output is reproducible from tracked source and is not committed.
